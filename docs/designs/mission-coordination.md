# Mission coordination

`missions.ts` defines typed checks and a diagnostic library of supported mission
candidates. `mission-chain.ts` validates recipes, worker levels, indexed resources,
tools, stations, owned funds and materials. Existing stock reduces new production.
A complete personal supply chain is tried before introducing teammate dependencies.

## Policy and selection

The shared-project policy currently filters eligible offers to the combat challenge;
regional expeditions and legacy skill/reserve templates remain in the diagnostic
library. This source policy is distinct from the active saved plan and operator
restrictions. Never restore a retired objective or retry a failed operator trial
merely because a template remains available.

`opportunities.ts` fingerprints relevant capabilities and stocks. Offers refresh on
changed facts or after five minutes; selection revalidates fresh all-party state.
The shortlist holds at most five candidates, with category diversity where possible.
An empty menu remains empty rather than inventing an unsupported mission.

Stable objective keys preserve checks across refresh. Normal councils reassign
work without replacing the mission. Selection follows absence, completion, explicit
rescope or structural infeasibility. Recoverable shortages are not automatic
retirement. Councils are normally hourly with a 15-minute request cooldown;
unchanged repeated requests can defer to the scheduled review.

## Ownership and evidence

The plan retains checks, owners, next actions and fallbacks. Retired unfinished goals
retain their reason and checks, bounded to ten retired missions. Coverage establishes
accountability, not proof that commitment prose is feasible or a delivery accepted.
Characters still choose and execute their own routines.

Automatic milestones require game facts or typed field/hunt proofs. Legacy freeform
milestones can close through two council claims, a weaker evidence class.
[Expedition](shared-expeditions.md) and [hunt](combat-hunts.md) proofs survive council
merges and are independent of learning-guide retention.

Readiness rechecks fresh levels, supplies and capacity. Remembered deterministic
blockers expire after 15 minutes or relevant fact changes; an unchanged bank refresh
does not clear missing materials. Feasibility cannot guarantee route safety, fresh
bank stock, shop availability, cooking success or future trade completion.

Tests cover empty/scarce menus, chains, ownership, stable keys, retired milestones
and fact-triggered invalidation. Live completion remains a separate observation.
