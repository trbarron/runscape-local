# Implementation contracts

These documents describe supported source behavior and known limits. They do not
establish deployment health or authorize changes to a live party. Active objectives,
budgets and operational evidence belong in the local handoff.

- [System](system-baseline.md): architecture, persistence and publication.
- [Missions](mission-coordination.md): feasibility, stable objectives and ownership.
- [Production](production-cycles.md): bounded own-bank continuation and reporting.
- [Supply](supply-coverage.md): dated stocks and replenishment advice.
- [Workshops](skill-workshops.md) and [ferry fishing](swordfish-fishing.md): supported chains.
- [Combat food](combat-provisions.md), [paired assist](paired-assist.md) and
  [hunt evidence](combat-hunts.md): readiness, consent and completion boundaries.
- [Learning](discovery-week.md) and [expeditions](shared-expeditions.md): observations,
  interpretations and mission evidence.
- [Recovery signals](progress-recovery.md) and [runtime continuity](runtime-continuity.md).
- [Scheduled maintenance](hourly-maintenance.md): optional external review.
- [Workflow](WORKFLOW.md), [feature template](templates/feature.md) and
  [review template](templates/review.md).
